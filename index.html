Upload a picture for every team, choose how many pieces, and run the race.

Step 1: how many pieces. Step 2: a picture for each team. Step 3: create the game.

Every team gets the same number of pieces. Each picture is cropped to fit the grid. During play, the full-colour reference image helps teams match the picture and piece edges.
A full-colour copy of each team picture will remain visible on the board as a placement hint.
Teams play one at a time. The team that finishes fastest wins. If nobody finishes, the team with the most pieces placed wins.
